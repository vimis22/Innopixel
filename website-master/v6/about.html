Handcrafting Pixels Since 2017

Innopixel ApS was founded in 2017 in Odense, Denmark by Michael Lundorff-Hansen. The vision arose from a clear frustration: advanced technologies like Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR) were too often treated as expensive novelty gimmicks rather than serious, purpose-driven solutions.

Michael wanted to change this by combining his deep experience in IT consulting, entrepreneurship, and academic teaching at the University of Southern Denmark (SDU) to establish a new kind of creative agency—a specialized production hub and talent collective.

Instead of maintaining a large, rigid staff of generalists, Innopixel operates as a highly agile network. We assemble top independent game developers, 3D environment artists, sound designers, and hardware engineers customized specifically for each project. This ensures our clients always get the brightest minds tailored exactly to their needs, while keeping development efficient and scalable.

We also believe strongly in building bridges to future innovators. We collaborate closely with SDU's Faculty of Engineering (SDU TEK) and Teknologiskolen, providing real-world project briefs, internships, and guest lectures to inspire and mentor the next generation of XR pioneers.
