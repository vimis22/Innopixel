Our Method

Crafting digital products

We follow a structured, collaborative workflow combining technical competence with artistic design.

01. Concept & Storytelling

Every project starts with a purpose. We work closely with museums, companies, and educators to frame the communication goals, storyboard the experience, and align on how design and technology will work together to tell a compelling story.

User Personas Storyboarding Interaction Design UX Flow Mapping

02. Rapid Prototyping

We believe in active experimentation. Instead of long planning cycles, we develop rough grey-box interactive prototypes in Unity or WebGL. This allows clients and stakeholders to put on the headsets or tap the screen and test the mechanics immediately.

Grey-boxing Unity URP WebGL Sketches Playtesting

03. Technical Architecture

Our team drafts robust code systems that scale. We implement 3D asset optimization pipelines, clean component-driven scripting, and connect physical hardware/sensors (like NFC chips or cameras) with responsive server infrastructure.

C# / Unity Engine Node.js Backends IoT Hardware APIs Shader Programming

04. Deployment & Launch

We configure the installation on site or publish the product globally. Whether setting up VR helmets at school classrooms, mounting short-throw projection systems on site, or deploying lightweight React/WebGL applications to high-availability web servers.

On-Site Calibration Standalone VR (Quest) Web Hosting Technical Training
