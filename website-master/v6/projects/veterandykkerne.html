A digital memorial exhibition and immersive 3D simulation documenting the perilous underwater missions of Denmark's pioneer commercial divers.

The Challenge

By creating a calm VR-based space, we aim to provide the restorative experience of diving along the coasts of Funen using 3D animation and 360-video, enhancing the therapeutic efforts of Veterandykkerne.
This application needed to simulate a safe and immersive diving environment to prepare users for real diving experiences and serve as a therapeutic tool to stabilize PTSD symptoms.

3D environments for an immersive experience

Project
responsibilities

While leaving workshops and the actual diving to our project partner, our responsibility is everything digital.
Creating a solid, easy user experience, a safe environment and accurate 3D-animation so users can explore these virtual waters, providing a sense of calm and preparation for actual diving.

Project goal

From the beginning of this collaboration, our goal has been to provide an experience worthy of our users. Our hope is that this project underscores the potential of digital technology in addressing mental health challenges and promoting well-being.

We’re excited to keep learning and exploring – if you have an idea for a similar project or you’re interested in Veterandykkerne and their work, please feel free to reach out to us.
